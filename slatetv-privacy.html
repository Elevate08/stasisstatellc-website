Slate TV Privacy Policy

Entity: Stasis State LLC • Effective Date: November 19, 2026 • Last Updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Slate TV Android TV launcher (com.stasisstate.slatetv) published by Stasis State LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). In short: Slate TV does not sell or share personal data, contains no ads or analytics, and collects nothing beyond Google Play order records for optional tips.

1. Data We Collect

Nothing except the Google Play order information for optional tips described in section 5. Slate TV has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not transmit data to any server operated by Stasis State LLC.

2. Installed Applications & Package Visibility

Because Slate TV functions as an Android TV home screen launcher, it queries Android's PackageManager to list, pin, reorder, hide, and launch your installed TV apps and (if you opt in) sideloaded apps, as well as to discover compatible Slate TV companion plugins.

Your installed-app inventory is processed strictly on your device and is never transmitted off your device, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling. Slate TV does not use an Accessibility Service.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your launcher preferences, pinned and hidden app dock layout, selected background source, and bounded local photo cache are stored solely on your device. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Clearing app data or uninstalling Slate TV removes all local data.

4. Optional Plugins & User-Configured Sources

Slate TV supports local photo folders and optional plugins or external sources that you choose to install and configure (such as personal photo servers or live TV program guides). Any credentials, local network requests, or media traffic for those sources stay between your device and the sources you configure. Stasis State LLC does not receive, inspect, or store this traffic.

5. Optional Tips

Tips are optional one-time in-app purchases processed entirely by Google Play over encrypted connections. They unlock nothing—every Slate TV feature is free without a purchase. We do not receive your payment card details; Google Play provides standard order metadata (such as order ID, product, and amount). Purchases are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

Network permissions are used only to load photos or shelf artwork from user-configured plugins/sources and to connect to Google Play Billing when you open Settings. Package visibility permissions are used solely to display and launch installed apps and discover plugins on your device.

7. Children

Slate TV is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will post any updates on this page and revise the "Last Updated" date.
