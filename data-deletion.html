User Account & Data Deletion

Stasis State LLC Data Governance • App-specific deletion instructions

Find deletion instructions for each Stasis State LLC application below. Survivor-X has its own deletion page at survivorx.stasisstate.com.

App Selection

This deletion policy applies to all accounts and services managed under Stasis State LLC, including:

  • Family Plates (Household meal planner, recipe organizer, and family synchronization)
  • Survivor-X (Arena survival game). Survivor-X has its own terms, privacy policy, and deletion page: see Survivor-X data deletion.

Family Plates: In-App Account Deletion

If you currently have the app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open Family Plates.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Account Management (or Profile).
  3. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Review the warning prompt and confirm the deletion.
  5. Your account and data will be immediately unlinked and queued for permanent purge.

Family Plates: Email Account Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion via the web, you can submit an official request directly to our data privacy team.

Submit Request via Email

Send an email from the address associated with your account:

  • Recipient: privacy@stasisstate.com
  • Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request - [Family Plates]
  • Body Information:
    • Applicable App: (Family Plates)
    • Registered Account Email / Username:
    • Confirmation: "I request permanent deletion of my account and associated personal data."
Draft Deletion Email

Family Plates: What Data Is Deleted?

Upon verifying and processing your deletion request, we permanently wipe the following data:

  • Authentication Credentials: Your email address, hashed passwords, session tokens, and linked identity providers.
  • Personal Profile Data: Display name, profile avatar, preferences, and notification tokens.
  • Application Content:
    • For Family Plates: Custom recipes, saved meal calendars, dietary restriction lists, and household associations.
  • Device Identifiers: Any advertising or hardware tokens associated with your account profile.

Family Plates: Retained Records

In certain strictly delimited circumstances, specific minimal records may be retained temporarily:

  • Financial & Purchase Records: If you made an in-app purchase via Google Play Billing, order transaction identifiers and timestamps are retained as required by tax laws, financial auditing regulations, and Google Merchant policies. These records do not contain sensitive credit card details.
  • Legal & Fraud Prevention Records: Minimal anonymized audit logs may be held for security, abuse detection, and statutory compliance under applicable law.

Family Plates: Processing Timeline

Once your identity is verified, your account and associated data are completely eradicated from production databases within 30 calendar days. Encrypted operational backups are cycled and purged according to our 60-day maximum backup retention schedule.

Questions and Assistance

If you encounter any issues requesting account or data deletion, please contact our support team at support@stasisstate.com.
