01 / Design & Features

Calm, Fast, Built for the Living Room

Everything runs inside one lightweight Android TV app engineered for instant wake recovery and clean D-pad navigation.

Gallery-First Home Screen

  • Full-screen rotating photo backdrops with smart portrait blur pillarbox and optional date/location captions
  • Distraction-free Idle Gallery Mode fades the dock and clock smoothly after inactivity
  • Built-in Ambient Slate gradients and Local Photos folder slideshow work completely offline

Effortless App Dock & All Apps

  • Pin, unpin, hide, and continuously reorder apps directly on the Home dock with your TV remote
  • Instant All Apps overlay to browse and launch every installed Android TV app (plus optional sideloaded apps)
  • Standard Android TV Home launcher with in-app Default Home setup—no Accessibility Service needed

Built-In Connectors & Live TV Guides

  • Connect self-hosted photo libraries over LAN discovery or QR phone pairing with Android Keystore credential encryption and TOFU SHA-256 TLS pinning
  • On-device Android TV Live Channels program guide and optional personal media server live TV shelf
  • Shelves stay completely hidden whenever unconfigured or unavailable

Zero-Code JSON Extensions & Wake Hardening

  • Add community photo feeds or home shelves via QR code or URL using the Open HTTP/JSON Extension Protocol—no extra APKs or code running on your TV
  • Handles HDMI-CEC wakes and SDR/HDR display mode changes in place without black flashes
  • Optional Tip Jar supports development and unlocks nothing; every feature is free
02 / Privacy & Permissions

Private by Architecture

Slate TV has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no servers operated by Stasis State LLC.

Installed Apps & Local Data: Because Slate TV is an Android TV home screen launcher, it reads installed app information strictly on-device so you can organize and launch your apps. Any optional photo library, live TV guide, or JSON feed traffic stays solely between your TV and the sources you configure.
