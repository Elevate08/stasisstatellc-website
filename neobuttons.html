01 / Capabilities

What NeoButtons Does

Built natively with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Material 3 for phones, tablets, and D-pad Android TV devices.

Hardware Keys & Custom Capture

  • Volume Up & Down, Headset / Media, Home, Back, Recent Apps, Camera, and Assistant keys
  • Live keycode & scancode capture for side keys, Bluetooth remotes, keyboards, gamepads, and Android TV remotes
  • Separate Single Tap, Double Tap, and Long Press triggers with adjustable delays

Navigation, Media & System Toggles

  • Navigation: Home, Back, Recent Apps, Last App, Notifications, Quick Settings, Power Menu, Lock Screen, Screenshot, Split Screen
  • Device controls: Flashlight (including screen-off), Auto-Rotate, Sound Mode, Do Not Disturb, Brightness, Switch Keyboard
  • Media & Volume: Play/Pause, Next, Previous, Stop, Seek, and Volume controls

Apps, Shortcuts & Automation

  • Launch any installed app, specific activity, camera, voice assistant, dialer, or deep link / URL
  • Broadcast custom intents with typed extras
  • Trigger HTTP webhooks (for smart-home integrations) and shell or root commands with live execution logging

Thoughtful Daily Controls

  • Pocket detection via proximity sensor and per-app bypass list (plus automatic pause during phone calls)
  • Optional rootless Screen-Off Volume Listener service, Quick Settings tile, and customizable haptic feedback
  • Light, Dark, and AMOLED Material You themes plus validated JSON backup & restore
02 / Transparency

Accessibility Service & Privacy

Designed around strict data minimization—your mappings and device activity never leave your device.

Accessibility Service Disclosure: NeoButtons uses the Android Accessibility Service API solely to detect physical hardware button presses and perform the actions you configure (such as Home, Back, Recent Apps, Screenshot, or Lock Screen), and reads the active app's package name only to pause remapping in apps on your bypass list. NeoButtons never reads screen content, never logs typed text, and never transmits Accessibility data off your device.
