NeoButtons Privacy Policy

Entity: Stasis State LLC • Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Last Updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the NeoButtons Android app (com.stasisstate.neobuttons) published by Stasis State LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). In short: NeoButtons does not sell or share personal data, and collects nothing beyond Google Play order records for optional tips.

1. Data We Collect

Nothing except the Google Play order information for optional tips described in section 5. NeoButtons has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not send data to any server we operate.

2. Accessibility Service

NeoButtons uses the Android Accessibility Service API to detect hardware button presses (such as volume, headset, remote and custom keys) and to perform the actions you assign to them, such as Home, Back, Recent Apps, Screenshot or Lock Screen. It also reads the package name of the app currently on screen, only to pause remapping in apps you add to its bypass list.

NeoButtons does not read the content of your screen, does not record what you type, and does not store or transmit any information obtained through the Accessibility Service. You are asked for consent before the service is enabled, and you can turn it off at any time in Android Settings.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your button mappings, settings and the in-app event log are stored only on your device. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If you export a backup file, it is saved where you choose and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

4. Actions You Configure

If you set up an action that opens a URL, calls an HTTP webhook (for example a Home Assistant server) or sends a broadcast intent, NeoButtons sends exactly what you configured to the destination you chose, when you press the button. We do not receive or see this traffic.

5. Optional Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases processed entirely by Google Play. They unlock nothing. We do not receive your payment details; Google Play provides us with standard order information such as the order ID, product and amount. Purchases are subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

Permissions such as notifications, Do Not Disturb access, Modify System Settings and Device Admin are used only to perform the actions you assign (for example toggling Do Not Disturb, changing brightness or locking the screen). Internet access is used only for the URL and webhook actions you configure and for Google Play Billing.

7. Children

NeoButtons is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the "Last Updated" date.
